Kevin Bean:
Geometry and Color

Organized by Bill Samios  ·  Curated by Bob Kelley

A memorial exhibition celebrating the life and work of painter Kevin Bean, a dear friend whose bold geometric abstractions brought joy and rigor in equal measure. Bob Kelley curated the show as a tribute to Bean's legacy and vision.
