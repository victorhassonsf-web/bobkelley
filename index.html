The Artist

Bob Kelley

Bob Kelley's relationship with photography began in 1977, in a science class where an assignment to create a camera-less image on light-sensitive paper — using a single freshly cut rose — produced something that felt like magic. Watching the silhouette emerge, he was captivated.

His love for flowers took root even earlier, when he moved into his grandmother's home. An eccentric and devoted woman, she wallpapered her rooms with dramatic floral patterns, and her gardens were expressions of care, beauty, and memory.

After art school, Kelley documented family events and rituals for decades. Family Flowers is drawn from a lifetime of looking closely at the people and places he loves most.
